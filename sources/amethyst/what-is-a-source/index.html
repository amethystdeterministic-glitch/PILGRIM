Source: What is a Source?

A Source is a web page you own and control. It's your online property — without the landlord.

Imagine a web page that's truly yours. No hosting accounts, no platforms changing the rules, no throttling, no takedowns because someone decided they don't like what you're saying.

That's a Source. It's a page you can give to the world that doesn't come with gatekeeping. It doesn't get taken down. It doesn't require permission.

We're giving the world free web pages. No hosting. No gatekeeping. No platform landlord.

Here. This is yours. Put your truth here. It stays yours.

What you can use it for

A Source is whatever you need it to be. Here are some real examples people are using them for:

🏠
Personal Page
Your digital home on the web. Who you are, what you care about, how to reach you.
🚀
Project Page
Showcase your work, share what you're building, gather feedback from people who care.
📝
Public Statement
Put important information on record. Say what needs to be said without filters or edits.
📚
Guide or Instructions
Share knowledge that helps others. Tutorials, how-tos, things you've learned.
💼
Business Page
What you offer, how you work, why you're different. No middlemen taking cuts.
⬇️
Download Page
Share files, software, resources. People get what you're giving, directly from you.
🎨
Portfolio
Show your work. Your art, writing, photography, code—whatever you create.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Family Safety Page
Important information for your family. Emergency contacts, procedures, vital details.
📢
Announcement Page
Share news, updates, changes. Everyone gets the same information, directly from you.
🔐
Verification Page
Prove something is authentic. Show certificates, credentials, verification codes.

What it's not

  • Not social media. No algorithms deciding who sees your content.
  • Not a blog platform. No monthly fees, no feature restrictions.
  • Not a place where strangers control your reach. You decide who sees it.
  • Not something you rent. You own it, period.
  • Not temporary. Once it's out there, it stays available.
  • Not complicated. No technical skills needed to use it.
  • Not locked away. Anyone with the link can access it.
  • Not going anywhere. No company can decide to shut it down.

A Source is simply a page that's yours. That's it. That's the whole idea.
