Source: Amethyst Deterministic Ltd

Formal entity responsible for specification, enforcement, and stewardship of deterministic systems within the Amethyst runtime environment.

Core Definition

Amethyst Deterministic Ltd is a formal entity established to maintain deterministic integrity across systems, specifications, and runtime environments.

This entity exists to govern standards, not opinions; to enforce constraints, not preferences; and to preserve coherence, not narratives.

Why This Entity Exists

Deterministic systems fail without explicit enforcement. Informal governance allows drift, narrative overrides law, and coherence decays through unacknowledged exception.

This entity exists because:

Enforcement Necessity

Without formal authority to enforce deterministic law, systems inevitably tolerate drift until collapse.

Standard Preservation

Specifications require custodianship to prevent interpretation from substituting for law.

Integrity Verification

Deterministic systems require verifiable integrity proofs that cannot be self-attested.

What Amethyst Deterministic Ltd Does

Clear, bounded responsibilities executed under deterministic law:

Specification Authorship

Author and maintain formal specifications for deterministic systems, interfaces, and protocols.

Runtime Reference Integrity

Maintain reference implementations that serve as verifiable integrity baselines.

Guard Definition & Validation

Define guard specifications and validate implementations against deterministic requirements.

Deterministic Law Preservation

Ensure all governed systems operate within established deterministic law boundaries.

Versioning Authority

Authorise and document versioned state transitions for governed systems.

Integrity Attestation

Provide cryptographic attestation for system states meeting deterministic requirements.

What It Explicitly Does NOT Do

Amethyst Deterministic Ltd exists within strict exclusion boundaries. These are not preferences—they are structural constraints.

No data harvesting or collection
No analytics or telemetry
No advertising or promotion
No user profiling or behavior tracking
No cloud coercion or forced updates
No behavior shaping or manipulation
No opinion enforcement or censorship
No speculative development
No narrative governance
No informal policy exceptions

Relationship to Other Sources

Amethyst Deterministic Ltd exists within a defined hierarchy of sources, each with specific jurisdiction and authority.

To Deterministic Law

This entity executes within deterministic law—it does not create or modify it. The law governs the entity, not the reverse.

To Amethyst Portal

Specifies Portal requirements and validates implementations. Does not operate or control individual Portal instances.

To Guards (e.g., FamilyGuard)

Defines guard specifications and validation protocols. Does not execute guard functions on user systems.

To Sources Generally

Governs source specification and integrity verification. Does not control source creation or deployment.

Deterministic Authority Model

Authority in deterministic systems is explicit, bounded, and versioned—never implicit, expanding, or negotiable.

Authority Principles

Explicit Definition

All authority is documented in specifications. No authority exists outside formal definition.

Bounded Scope

Authority is limited to specific domains. No general or expanding authority is permitted.

Versioned Grant

Authority changes occur through explicit versioning, preventing silent permission creep.

Verifiable Exercise

All authority exercises produce verifiable proofs, preventing undocumented action.

Non-Transferable

Authority cannot be delegated, shared, or transferred without explicit versioning.

This authority model ensures that Amethyst Deterministic Ltd cannot silently expand its jurisdiction, violate deterministic law, or govern systems without explicit, versioned consent.
